How the film was made

  • 54 seconds, 1920×1080, English on-screen text, no narration.
  • Every number on screen comes from the project's public tables, which passed its publish gate. The map is the project's public map.
  • The points and parcels in the pairing and comparison shots illustrate the rule; they are not real records.
  • The music was synthesised in code by this project, with no samples and no generative model.
  • The film was produced with the help of AI tools. Every number on screen was checked against the public tables.
